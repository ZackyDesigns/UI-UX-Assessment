Design Rationale

SD

Sipho Dlamini

28-year-old Software Developer • R25,000/month • Master's Student

Context & Challenges: Between work, studying, raising his young son, and paying off student debt, he has almost no free-time. He wants to build his credit score, save consistently, start investing, and eventually qualify for a home loan, while also building financial security for his son's future.

The Core Problem: Sipho knows what he wants to achieve financially but struggles to know what should take priority. His money is spread across different accounts and responsibilities, leaving him feeling overwhelmed by which decisions to take. Nova helps him prioritise by presenting his financial health in one place and recommending the most important action to take.

01. Control Room Dashboard

The home page was designed to act as the user's control room. Everything users need most is accessible from a single screen, including Savings, Investments, Credit, and Expenses. The goal was to let users understand where they stand and what they should focus on without navigating through multiple pages.

02. Visuals & Color Psychology

To reduce anxiety, colours commonly associated with danger or caution, such as red, orange, and yellow, were avoided. Instead, navy blue was chosen as the primary colour because it is often associated with trust and stability. White was kept as the dominant background colour to maintain the familiarity users already expect from fintech applications, while a neomorphic design gives the UI a modern feel while still feeling familiar.

03. Encouraging Micro-Copy

The wording throughout the application avoids negative connotations. Rather than making users feel like they have done something wrong, the app focuses on providing solutions and encouraging positive habits. Completing a recommended action provides immediate positive feedback, reinforcing progress instead of highlighting mistakes.

Key Feature

Financial Pulse & Focused Attention

  • Focused Attention: To attract the user's attention without overwhelming them, only one notification or animation appears at a time. The most urgent task is always highlighted first, prompting the user towards the action that will have the most impact on their financial well-being.
  • Financial Pulse Indicator: The focal point of the home screen Financial Pulse, inspired by a heart rate monitor. Rather than showing several separate scores, it gives users an indicator of their overall financial health based on their savings, expenses, credit, investments, and cash flow.
  • Action-Oriented Navigation: If the Pulse begins to slow, the app directs users to the section that needs attention, helping keep the home screen clean while still making important information easy to find.
